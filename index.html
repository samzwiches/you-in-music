Practical reassurance

Questions people ask right before they trust something tender.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. The site should help people discover the shape of it as they answer.

Can this be a happy song too?

Yes. Celebration, devotion, confidence, gratitude, and gift songs should all feel native here.

When does payment happen?

After the emotional doorway and before the deeper story prompt opens.

Can I buy full rights?

Yes. Rights stay reserved by default and can be upgraded when needed.

Can this be for someone else?

Absolutely. Gifts, tributes, birthdays, vows, and thank-you songs all deserve their own lane.

What if I cannot explain the feeling well?

That is the point of the intake. It should meet people before they become eloquent.

Will my story stay private?

Yes. The site should repeat that promise in plain language, not only in fine print.

How long does it take?

Delivery varies by tier, but the page should always lead with clarity rather than speed theater.
