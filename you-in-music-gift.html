Best gift tiers
  • Spark for birthdays, praise songs, and bright fast gifts
  • Deep Dive for thank-you songs, apologies, and emotionally rich surprises
  • Signature Piece for weddings, tributes, and forever gifts
Gift flow notes
  • Let the buyer describe the recipient in their own language
  • Offer a dedication note field after payment
  • Let them choose whether the song goes to them first or directly to the recipient
  • Gift-ready delivery is a real upgrade, not fluff
