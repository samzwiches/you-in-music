Process

The practical page that should still feel human.

This is where delivery, rights, revisions, and privacy live. It should answer real questions without snapping the emotional spell of the brand.

01
Emotional doorway

Song path + checkout

03
Private delivery

The finished piece arrives quietly with the promised turnaround.

Rights
  • Rights stay reserved by default
  • Full rights buyout is an optional upgrade
  • Preview links can be watermarked if needed
Delivery
  • Spark: around 3 days
  • Deep Dive: around 5 days
  • Signature Piece: around 7 to 10 days
  • Rush delivery can be offered when it makes sense
Privacy promise

The intake should explicitly say that their story is treated like a confidence. Private details should not be turned into social proof without permission.

Revision promise

Keep revisions simple and tier-based so the visitor knows what kind of collaboration they are buying.

You In Music
