HOW A STORY BECOMES A SONG

You bring the truth.
We find the music hiding in it.

No songwriting degree. No perfect paragraph. No need to organize your life into bullet points before you arrive.

THE ACTUAL PROCESS

Messy in. Meaningful out.

01
FOLLOW THE FEELING

Start wherever your brain naturally starts.

A person. A memory. A sound. A sentence. We do not make you fill out a tax form for your emotions.

02
FIND THE EMOTIONAL SPINE

Figure out what the song is really trying to say.

Under the dates and stories there is usually one central truth. That becomes the backbone.

03
KEEP THE HUMAN DETAILS

Names, habits, places, weird phrases and all.

The specific details are what keep a personal song from feeling mass produced.

04
SHAPE THE SOUND

Build a musical world around the story.

Tender, huge, nostalgic, dark, bright, cinematic, raw. The sound should do emotional work too.

05
BRING IT HOME

You get a song that belongs to this exact life.

Private by default. Keep it close, give it away, and decide for yourself what ever leaves the room.

WHAT MAKES IT PERSONAL?

Not just the topic.
The fingerprints.

Not: “a song for my mom”

More like: “a song for the woman who burned every first pancake, danced barefoot while cleaning, and called me exactly three minutes after every storm warning.”

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your story stays private unless you say otherwise.

You decide what stays between you and the song. Nothing becomes public just because you made it here.
