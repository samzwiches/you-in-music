Start Your Song

Let's find your sound.

Start with a sound that feels like you. Then share the people, memories, and little details that belong in your song.

Step 1

How should your song feel?

Choose the feeling that comes closest. You can change your mind.

Step 2

Choose the chapter this belongs to.

Celebration
Birthday, graduation, or glow-up

For the moments that should sound like a camera flash and a real heartbeat at once.

Relationship
Love, vows, devotion, or apology

For the people who matter too much for generic language.

Healing
Closure, survival, or reinvention

For rebuilding yourself without sounding like a motivational poster.

Tribute
Memory, grief, or thank you

For the songs that need more care than speed.

Step 3

Let them say who it belongs to.

Start with the spark
  • Find the emotional doorway
  • Choose a sound that feels right
  • Name who this song belongs to
  • Follow the thread that makes it personal
Then make it unmistakably yours
  • Tell us the story behind it
  • Add names, dates, memories, and tiny details
  • Choose how deep you want to go
  • Receive it privately when it is ready
You found the feeling. Now give it somewhere to go.

Pick the song experience that fits your story, your moment, and how much of yourself you want woven into it.
