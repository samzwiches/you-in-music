Direction

Palette and voice

Keep the site cinematic, not fantasy-costume. The magic should feel intimate and lived in, with enough warmth to keep the darker colors from flattening into one note.

Color blend

Black and indigo are the floor. Aged gold is the guiding light. Dusty rose keeps the emotion human. Soft teal cools it off so the whole thing does not become an all-purple fog.

Voice rules
Say less, but mean more. Use invitation instead of explanation. Let practical details live lower on the page. Never sound like a generic AI music service. Every line should feel like it knows why the visitor came.
